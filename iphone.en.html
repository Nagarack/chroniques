Chroniques on iPhone and iPad

No App Store needed: Chroniques installs on the home screen and opens like an app, full screen, with notifications (“Your turn!”, session reminder).

Install (once)

  1. Open the campaign’s HTTPS address sent by your GM (link or QR code), preferably in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share ⬆︎ button (a square with an arrow pointing up), at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, at the top on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  4. Open Chroniques from its new icon and sign in.
  5. For notifications: Settings → Session reminder → Enable, then accept the iPhone’s request.
Good to know
  • Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later, and only work from the home screen icon (not in a Safari tab).
  • On iOS 17 and later, Chrome and other browsers also offer “Add to Home Screen” in their share menu.
  • The address must be the campaign’s HTTPS one: the GM’s PC local address (127.0.0.1) does not work from a phone.
  • Sign in without a password: Settings → Passkeys → “Create a key on this device” (Face ID or Touch ID).
  • Language: My account → English / Français.
  • Updates: nothing to do, the app updates itself with the GM’s server.
