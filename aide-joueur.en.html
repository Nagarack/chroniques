Player cheat sheet

Chroniques tracks your character during the game. Dice always stay physical: the app rolls nothing, you simply record the results.

Joining the game

  • Scan the GM’s QR code, or open the link they send you: the code is already filled in.
  • Choose a username and a password (12 characters minimum).
  • Already have an account? Sign in first, then open the link.
  • On a phone: “Add to Home Screen” to open it like an app, or, on Android, the Chroniques app. iPhone: see the installation guide.
  • 🔑 No password: Settings → Passkeys → “Create a key on this device”; then “Sign in with a passkey” is enough (browser, not the Android app).
  • 🌐 Language: My account → English / Français.
  • Network down: your HP, resources, spell slots and potions drunk are noted on the device and sent when the network is back.

During your turn

  • The “Your turn!” banner appears at the top of the screen.
  • Sheet: Action, Bonus action, Reaction and Movement available.
  • Roll your dice, announce the result, let the GM decide.

Hit points

  • −10 … +10 buttons to record damage and healing.
  • First choose the damage type: your resistances are applied automatically.
  • At 0 HP: record your death saving throws (success / failure).
  • Temporary HP: they are removed first.

Spells and items

  • 📓 Personal notebook (sheet, In play): your secrets, which only the GM can read.
  • Offline: the page opens with the latest data; your HP and notes are sent when the network is back.
  • Game rules: all the SRD rules, with search (“prone”, “cover”). Clicking a condition (Prone, Grappled…) shows its rule.
  • 📖 Description in the spellbook: the full text of each spell, also printed on the spell cards.
  • Companion, familiar, mount: add it on your sheet (In play tab); “Send to combat” gives it its turn right after you; manage its HP and place its token on the map.
  • Export my character (Progression tab) to reuse it in another campaign.
  • Spellbook: prepared spells, remaining slots, concentration.
  • Inventory: items, quantities, coins, and the 🧰 party chest (“Give to… → Party chest” to deposit, “Take” to take back).
  • ✎ History (sheet): everything that happened to your character, recorded automatically.
  • Short and long rests from the sheet.

The party

  • ✉ Whispers (at the top): private messages with the GM, invisible to the other players.
  • Quest objectives and the date in the world appear in Journal & quests and on the home page.
  • Shared notes: a notebook shared by all the players. The GM reads it without editing it.
  • Journal: places, quests, discoveries and session summaries shared by the GM.
  • Maps: drag your token; markers, chests and areas revealed by the GM.
  • Forgotten password: ask the GM for a reset link.
  • A problem? My account → Report a problem.
  • In combat: the home page shows your HP, your actions and your potions; your phone vibrates when it’s your turn.
  • 📱 Android app: Settings → download the app (same features as the website).
  • 🔔 Session reminder: Settings → “Enable on this device” to be notified the day before, and on your turn in combat.
  • Ctrl+K or the magnifying glass: find everything.

Shortcuts

  • ? screen help · / search · Esc close
  • Conditions (Frightened, Prone…) are explained when you hover over or tap their name.
  • “Connection lost” in red: wait for the network to come back before clicking.

To print: Ctrl + P on a computer, or Share → Print on a phone.
