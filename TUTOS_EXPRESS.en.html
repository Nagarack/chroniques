Quick tutorials

Each section is explained in three short steps. Choose your role, then open only the topic you need. On the site, the matching help is folded at the top of each screen.

To begin: the GM creates the campaign and the invitations; the player joins with their code and opens their sheet. Always roll physical dice. For a demo, start with test accounts and campaigns, never personal data.

For players

Open a section to show three steps. The same explanations are available directly on each screen of the app.

Accueil et bureau3 steps
  1. Check the name of the active campaign, at the very top of the screen.
  2. Open a desk panel or a shortcut to reach what you are looking for.
  3. The Help button offers a complete path, to follow at your own pace.
Character sheets3 steps
  1. Choose your character and see their HP, armour class and resources.
  2. Open In play, Actions & resources, Progression & rules or History as needed.
  3. Record damage and healing with the −10 to +10 buttons, after your real rolls; choose the damage type if you know it.
Combat3 steps
  1. Roll your real dice and announce your initiative to the table.
  2. See the turn order, actions and conditions of your character.
  3. Record the resources spent and the damage after the GM’s approval.
Cartes et repères3 steps
  1. Choose a map available in the campaign.
  2. Drag your token to show where your character is.
  3. Tap a marker to see its name; dark areas have not been explored yet.
Inventaire et trésors3 steps
  1. “Drink” a potion: roll the dice shown and enter the total to heal yourself.
  2. Equip armour and shield then apply the suggested AC; keep an eye on load and attunements.
  3. Buy from merchants opened by the GM, give an item to another player or put it in the party chest.
Scènes et séances3 steps
  1. Open the visible scenes in the active campaign.
  2. Find the objectives and summaries shared by the GM.
  3. Check the log to remember important decisions.
Spellbook3 steps
  1. Choose your character and see the spells known or prepared.
  2. Check the range, conditions and available spell slots.
  3. Make the rolls with your physical dice and record the resources spent.
Journal et mémoire3 steps
  1. Give your availability for the next sessions and read the handouts sent by the GM.
  2. Search for an event, or filter decisions and consequences.
  3. You only see the information the GM has shared.
Illustrations3 steps
  1. Choose the category: portrait, creature or item.
  2. Open the image to see it larger.
  3. If an image is missing, ask the GM to import it again.
Campagnes et accès3 steps
  1. Open My campaigns to see the tables you take part in.
  2. Choose Open, or use the Campaign selector, to change table.
  3. To join another table, open the link or scan the QR code sent by its GM.
Settings3 steps
  1. Check the D&D edition used by the campaign.
  2. GM-only settings are not accessible to players.
  3. If there is a problem, tell the GM your device and what you were doing.
Guided path3 steps
  1. Choose a step of the player path and open the suggested screen.
  2. Try the steps with your group, without rolling dice in the app.
  3. Tick “I have finished”; you can start the path again at any time.

For the GM and co-GM

Open a section to show three steps. The same explanations are available directly on each screen of the app.

Accueil et bureau3 steps
  1. Choose the right campaign and check that you are in Game Master mode.
  2. Click Customise to show, hide or resize panels.
  3. Move the panels with the handle or the arrows; the order is remembered on this device.
Character sheets3 steps
  1. Select an adventurer and check their HP, statistics and resources.
  2. Choose the damage type before recording it: resistances and vulnerabilities apply on their own.
  3. Check every change with the player: dice stay physical.
Combat3 steps
  1. Add creatures from the SRD bestiary: several copies share a single initiative.
  2. After the real rolls, enter the initiatives then start combat; N moves to the next turn, U undoes.
  3. The action buttons record the attack and remind you of the dice; the GM screen summarises the rules.
Cartes et repères3 steps
  1. Import a map, add a grid and tokens for the adventurers or creatures.
  2. Keep secret markers private; enable the fog then tap the map to reveal explored areas.
  3. For a chest: prepare the loot, reveal it, then assign each item to the players.
Inventaire et trésors3 steps
  1. Choose a character to check their equipment and charges.
  2. Prepare treasures, share the loot gold, open the merchants prepared in the GM Library.
  3. Assign an item and its quantity; check the remaining stock and the character’s inventory.
Scènes et séances3 steps
  1. Create a scene with a title, a summary and GM-only notes.
  2. Link it to an existing quest, place, NPC or encounter.
  3. Set the scene to “In progress” then “Completed”; reveal only the summary you want.
Spellbook3 steps
  1. Open the spellbook of the character concerned.
  2. Check prepared spells, slots and concentration.
  3. Let the players roll their dice, then record the resources spent.
Encounters3 steps
  1. Create your homebrew monsters and items, or start from an SRD sheet.
  2. Prepare the encounter and give the items at the right time, without publishing your secret notes.
  3. During the game, open Combat to manage turns and conditions.
Préparation des scénarios3 steps
  1. Give a title and a hook; use a starting lead if needed.
  2. Set the stakes, the secrets and three key moments of the adventure.
  3. Link places, scenes and NPCs, then save your draft before playing.
Journal et mémoire3 steps
  1. Proofread the automatic session summary, then share it or add it to the memory.
  2. Choose “GM private” or “Visible to players” for each entry.
  3. Mark the story threads “Resolved” or “To follow” before the next session.
Illustrations3 steps
  1. Choose a portrait, an item, a monster or a marker in the media library.
  2. Reuse a saved image or import a new illustration.
  3. Check how it looks on the sheet or the map before revealing it.
Campagnes et accès3 steps
  1. Choose the campaign in My campaigns or with the selector at the top.
  2. Click “Invite this player”: have the QR code scanned or send the link, the code is already inside.
  3. Owner: appoint a co-GM (full or “creatures”) in role management; test the Player view in My view.
Settings3 steps
  1. Check the rules edition and the campaign settings.
  2. Back up the data before a major update.
  3. For the beta, report problems with the role, the screen, the device and the action performed.
Guided path3 steps
  1. Open the GM path to check campaign, invitations, maps and combat.
  2. Have the player path tried from another account, without exposing private notes.
  3. Use each section’s quick help during testing.

Roles are checked by the server. Secret notes, private images and campaigns are never included in these tutorials.
