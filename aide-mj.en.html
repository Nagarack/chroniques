Game Master cheat sheet

The server runs on your PC: keep the DEMARRER_CHRONIQUES window open during the game. Dice stay physical.

Before the session

  1. Campaign → Invite this player (or the whole table): a QR code to scan, or a link to send.
  2. Characters: check HP, AC, resistances and resources with each player.
  3. Combat → Monsters and NPCs: add the creatures (SRD bestiary or free sheet).
  4. GM Library: your homebrew monsters and items, your encounters and their difficulty for the party’s level.
  5. Today’s session (Preparation): scenes, encounters, NPCs, quests and handouts on one printable page.
  6. 📜 Previously on… (Today’s session): compose, proofread, publish; can be shown on the table screen.
  7. 🧭 Travel (Journal & quests → Calendar): distance, pace, camp, rations, lights.
  8. ⚜ Factions & relationships (Notebook): party reputation from −5 to +5 with each faction, members, NPC ↔ character relationships.
  9. ⚡ Quick NPC (Journal & quests → Recurring NPCs): name, role, trait, voice, secret, with ideas to pick from.
  10. 🎲 Random tables (Today’s session): write “1-3: …”, roll your die, enter the total; “Announce to players” adds it to their log.
  11. ♫ Scene ambience (Prepare the session): the chosen track starts when the scene becomes “in progress”.
  12. ✓ Table charter (Game tracking): house rules (optional reminder in the GM screen), lines and veils, session zero notes. Remind everyone of the X-card: an anonymous signal reaches you as a banner.
  13. ⏳ Downtime (Today’s session): grant days to the party; each player spends them on their activities.
  14. Back up: “Back up now” button or SAUVEGARDER_CHRONIQUES.bat.

During combat

  • 📖 Sheet among an SRD creature’s actions: its official stat block.
  • ✉ Whispers: a private message to one player (“you notice…”) that the others don’t see.
  • Game rules and clickable conditions: the official rule in two clicks.
  • Enter the initiatives rolled with dice, then Start combat.
  • N next turn · U undo the last action (10 levels).
  • Condition durations: end or start of turn, caster’s turn, saving throw reminder at the end of each turn (⏳ on the condition).
  • Party view: saving throws, passive Perception and spell save DCs for the whole table. Optional turn timer ⏱.
  • Co-GM station (/#poste-comj): a screen to run the creatures on a tablet; “Creature co-GM” role for a friend with no access to your secrets.
  • Choose the damage type: resistance ÷ 2, vulnerability × 2, immunity 0.
  • 💥 Area damage (Combat desk, or “💥 Damage” under an area placed on the map): dice total, “succeeded” or “failed” for each target; half or nothing depending on the spell, then resistances. U undoes.
  • Concentration (adventurers and creatures): the saving throw DC is shown with each damage.
  • Creatures in groups: a single initiative; action buttons and a legendary action counter under each creature.
  • ⚑ Ready an action (trigger, then “Trigger” = reaction) and ⏸ Delay (table option) on the active participant’s line.
  • ✦ Heroic Inspiration: “✦ Inspire” on the party cards or on the sheet.
  • 🗳 Table vote and ✉ message in a language (Today’s session); ❔ mysterious item in the inventory; 🦠 disease or poison, ✝ memorial on the sheet.
  • ⬡ Hexes (Maps → “Hexes” grid): exploration and party travel. 👁 Spectator link in Settings.
  • ☁ Today’s weather (Chronicle → Calendar): seen by players, the table screen and the spectator. Health states of creatures with hidden HP (checkbox in the initiative order). “Follow world time” lighting in token vision: day from 6 am to 8 pm.
  • ⏳ Clocks (Today’s session): a threat or a project in 4 to 12 segments, visible or secret. ⭐ Stars and wishes (Chronicle → Sessions): anonymous end-of-session feedback. 🔎 Investigation board (Chronicle → Investigation): clues to reveal and link. ✏ Draw on maps, and token altitude.
  • 🎭 Stage it (Session scenes): image, read-aloud text and ambience on the table screen, for players and the spectator. Start an encounter on a map (GM library): tap the map and the tokens are placed in formation. 💡 Suggestions: anyone can suggest an improvement from My account; the server administrator sorts them in Administration.
  • 📱 Shared table mode (My account): a shared tablet where each player taps their character; your 4–8 digit code is needed to leave it. 📜 Travel journal (Chronicle → Journeys). Administration: Server (accounts, campaigns, storage, backups) and Reports tabs; ideas can be sorted by support.
  • 🌙 Moons (Chronicle → Calendar → “Moons”): up to 3 moons, the phase shown under the date, full moons announced in the log. 📍 Ping on a map (Alt+click or long press): a ripple the whole table sees. Administration → Server → Accounts: new password link, sign out everywhere, suspend or reactivate an account. 🦘 Jumping and falling (sheet → Actions & resources).
  • 🧱 Walls and doors (Maps → “Walls and doors”): vision stops at walls; click a door to open it. ⚠ Traps: state and DCs on “Trap” markers.
  • 🎲 Group check (Combat or Today’s session): everyone enters their real d20, automatic verdict.
  • 👁 Token vision (Maps, fog on): the fog lifts around the adventurers; lit area or darkness, light of each token.
  • GM screen: common rules, from the combat.
  • Table screen: map and initiative for the TV, with no secrets.
  • Ctrl+K: search everywhere and run a command.

What players see

  • Hidden creatures: invisible to them. Visible ones: name and conditions only.
  • Monster statistics, defences and notes: never sent.
  • Shared notes: written by the players, readable by you.
  • Character history: your secret notes never appear there for them.
  • “My view → Player” to check their point of view.

Updates and backups

  • Update: Settings → “Update Chroniques” → upload the ZIP → “Install and restart” (server started with DEMARRER_CHRONIQUES.bat). Updates published on GitHub are also offered automatically.
  • Restore a backup: Settings → choose a date → “Test” (nothing is changed) then “Restore…”. The current state is backed up just before.
  • Mirror copy: enter a USB stick or OneDrive / Google Drive folder in Settings; every backup is copied there. A daily backup is made automatically.
  • Campaign template (Settings): export / import your preparation (random tables included).
  • 🔑 Passkeys (Settings → Passkeys): sign in with your fingerprint, face or device code. A key created on the HTTPS address only works on that address; your password stays valid.
  • 🌐 Language: My account → English / Français. Each person chooses; the campaign data does not change.
  • Or close the server, then drag the new ZIP onto METTRE_A_JOUR.bat. Backup, check and rollback are automatic.
  • A problem? REVENIR_VERSION_PRECEDENTE.bat.
  • Copy the backups folder somewhere else from time to time.

Playing remotely

  • CONFIGURER_URL_HTTPS.bat saves the Cloudflare tunnel address.
  • Without an HTTPS address, invitation links only work on your PC.
  • PROTEGER_CLOUDFLARE_ACCESS.bat to restrict access to the site.

Shortcuts

  • ? screen help · / search · Esc close
  • Guided tour: Help & tutorial → Start the tour.
  • Practice campaign: My campaigns, to test without risk.

To print: Ctrl + P.
