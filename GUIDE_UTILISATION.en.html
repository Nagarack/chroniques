Chroniques • Table guide

User guide

Need a short explanation? See the quick tutorials (three steps per screen, for the GM and the player). They are also folded at the top of each section of the app. Version française (more detailed).

Character sheets, combats, sessions and campaign journal for D&D 5e. This guide is for Game Masters and players. All dice are rolled physically around the table: Chroniques never rolls anything, you record the results. Rules texts in English come from the official SRD 5.1 (CC BY 4.0). Distances in the interface are in metres (1 square = 1.5 m = 5 ft).

1. Signing in

Game Master
  1. Run DEMARRER_CHRONIQUES.bat on the PC hosting Chroniques.
  2. Open the campaign’s HTTPS site or the local address.
  3. Sign in to the GM account.
  4. Check the sheets in “Campaign”.
Player
  1. Open the address given by the GM, or scan their QR code.
  2. Go through Cloudflare Access if enabled.
  3. Choose “I have an invitation” the first time (the code is already filled in from the link).
  4. Create your account, then sign in.

2. Language, phones and passkeys

  1. Language: My account → English / Français (also on the sign-in screen). By default, Chroniques follows your device’s language. Each person chooses: the campaign data does not change, so a French-speaking GM and English-speaking players can share a table.
  2. Android: Settings → Android app, then install the APK (Google may warn because it is not from the Play Store: “More details” → “Install anyway”).
  3. iPhone / iPad: open the HTTPS address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. See the iPhone guide.
  4. Passkeys: Settings → Passkeys → “Create a key on this device”, then sign in with your fingerprint, face or device code. Not available in the Android app, nor on a numeric address like 127.0.0.1 (use http://localhost:8080 on the GM’s PC).
  5. Offline: HP, resources, spell slots, items used and notes made without a network are sent when it comes back.

3. The interface

On a computer, the main entries stay visible: Dashboard, Combat, Characters and Maps. Open Game tracking, Preparation or Library & settings for the other tools. On a phone, the bottom bar shows Home, Sheet, Combat, More and Help; the header shows “My view” (Game Master / Player) and the ☰ account menu.

Ctrl+K (or the magnifying glass) searches everywhere: characters, monsters, items, spells, maps, handouts and commands. Keyboard: ? screen help, / search, Esc close; in combat, for the GM, N next turn and U undo.

4. At the table

  1. Invite a player: Campaign → Player invitations → Invite this player (or the whole table). QR code or link, valid 24 h, single use.
  2. Shared notes: a notebook all players can write; the GM reads it without editing.
  3. Whispers ✉: private messages between the GM and one player.
  4. Resistances and vulnerabilities: set them on each sheet or monster, then choose the damage type before recording damage: resistance ÷ 2, vulnerability × 2, immunity 0.
  5. Condition durations (GM): removed automatically at the chosen time, with a saving throw reminder at the end of each turn.
  6. Undo: “↩ Undo” goes back over the last 10 combat actions.

5. Preparing (GM)

  1. Today’s session (Preparation): scenes, encounters, NPCs, quests and handouts on one printable page, with “Previously on…” to compose and publish.
  2. Scenes: title, summary for players, secret notes, links; a scene can start an ambience track when it becomes “in progress”.
  3. GM Library: homebrew monsters (full stat block or copied from the SRD), homebrew items, merchants, prepared encounters with their difficulty.
  4. Random tables (Today’s session): write lines like “1-3: Three goblins”, roll your real die, enter the total: the line is shown. “Announce to players” adds it to their log.
  5. Campaign notebook: places, NPCs, factions (party reputation −5 to +5), quests with objectives; Quick NPC for an NPC in one minute.
  6. Travel (Journal & quests → Calendar): distance, pace, forced march, camp, rations and lights; the world calendar advances with rests and travel.
  7. Campaign template (Settings): export / import your preparation to replay it with another group.

6. Running a combat

  1. Add creatures: Combat → Monsters and NPCs (SRD bestiary, your bestiary or a free sheet). Several copies share one initiative.
  2. Enter the initiatives rolled with real dice, then Start combat; Next turn (N) moves on.
  3. Under each creature: action buttons (they record the action and remind you of the dice), legendary actions, recharges, spell slots, concentration.
  4. 💥 Area damage: one dice total, “succeeded / failed” per target, half or nothing depending on the spell, then resistances. From the Combat desk or from an area placed on the map (targets touched are pre-ticked).
  5. Table screen: map and initiative on a TV, with no secrets. Co-GM station (/#poste-comj) to run the creatures from a tablet.
  6. Players see their HP, actions, slots and potions as big buttons; their phone vibrates on their turn.

7. Maps

  1. Campaign maps: import an image, add a grid, place tokens for adventurers and creatures (drag them).
  2. Fog of war: tap the map to reveal areas. Markers and chests stay secret until you reveal them.
  3. Measure distances and place spell areas (circle, cone, line, cube); the list shows who is touched.
  4. Share the map with the players when ready: its entire background image becomes visible.

8. Character sheet

  1. In play: HP (−10 … +10 after your real rolls), temporary HP, death saving throws, rests, conditions, personal notebook, companions.
  2. Actions & resources: features and counters; a bonus action only if a feature allows it.
  3. Progression & rules: abilities, proficiencies, species, background, level up (guided), spells and feats.
  4. History: everything that happened to the character, recorded automatically; the GM can add notes (secret ones are never sent to the player).
  5. Spellbook: prepared spells, casting a spell spends the chosen slot, concentration starts automatically; 📖 shows the full official text.
  6. Inventory: drink a potion (roll the dice shown), equip armour and shield for the AC, give items, party chest, merchants opened by the GM.

9. Several campaigns, GM or player view

  1. My campaigns: create a campaign, join another table with an invitation, switch with the Campaign selector at the top.
  2. Permissions are per campaign: the same person can be GM at one table and player at another.
  3. My view (GM / co-GM): switch to Player to check what the players see; the server blocks GM commands in that view.
  4. Co-GM roles: full co-GM (everything) or “creature co-GM” (runs the monsters only, no access to your notes).

10. Backups and updates

  1. A daily backup is made automatically; Settings → Backups to test and restore one (the current state is saved just before). Add a mirror folder (USB stick, OneDrive) to copy each backup.
  2. Updates published by the developer are offered in Settings → Update: “Install and restart”. Or close the server and drag the ZIP onto METTRE_A_JOUR.bat. If something goes wrong, the previous version comes back automatically; REVENIR_VERSION_PRECEDENTE.bat also restores it.
  3. Security: the server only listens on 127.0.0.1; remote players come through your Cloudflare HTTPS tunnel, with Chroniques accounts (and optionally Cloudflare Access).

11. New in 5.3

  1. Multiclassing: “Level up” → “Class that gains this level”: a current class or a new one (13 prerequisites checked; the GM may allow an exception). Sheet → Progression & rules → Classes: levels per class, subclasses, hit dice by type, proficiencies gained. Spell slots follow the multiclass rule (combined caster level); Pact Magic stays separate.
  2. Heroic Inspiration: the GM taps “✦ Inspire” on a party card or “Grant” on the sheet; the player chooses “Use” or “Give to”. Ready an action (trigger + action, then “Trigger” as a reaction) and Delay (table option) on the active turn.
  3. Table charter: house rules (shared or GM only, reminder in the GM screen), session zero notes, lines and veils added anonymously, anonymous X-card and pause request.
  4. Downtime: Today’s session → grant days to the party; each player spends them on activities (crafting, training, work, research, recovery) on their sheet.
  5. Token vision: Maps → fog on → “👁 Token vision”: the fog lifts around adventurers as they move (lit area or darkness, darkvision, token light). No walls: adjust the fog by hand if needed. Distances: My account → metres or feet.

12. New in 5.4

  1. Walls and doors: Maps → “Walls and doors”. Tap a starting point then the next points (tap the same point twice to stop). Choose “Door” to draw a door: a small button on the map opens or closes it. Token vision stops at walls and closed doors.
  2. Traps: create a marker with the “Trap” icon, then set it up (SRD example, DCs, effect). Hidden / Spotted / Disarmed / Triggered; “Spotted” or “Triggered” shows it to the players, without the DCs.
  3. Group checks and saves: Combat or Today’s session → “Group check or save”. Players enter their d20 total on their home page; “End” writes the result to the log.
  4. Recharges: set “Charge recharge” and “Charges regained” on an item (e.g. 1d6+1, empty = all). After the rest, “Charges to regain” appears on the sheet. Madness: on the sheet (GM). Spells per class: in a multiclassed character’s spellbook. Calendar: “📅 Add to my calendar” on the next session card.

13. New in 5.5

  1. Votes: Today’s session or Combat → “Table vote”; one answer per line. Players vote from their home page. Languages: each player notes their languages (sheet → Progression & rules); the GM sends a “Message in a language” from Today’s session.
  2. Mysterious items: in a character’s inventory, “❔ Mysterious item (GM)”: true name, true description, curse; “Identify” reveals it. Diseases and poisons: on the sheet (GM); the player records saving throws, the GM taps “Cured”.
  3. Memorial: sheet → “✝ Add to the memorial”. Statistics: Chronicle. Hexes: Maps → “Hexes” grid → “Hexes” tool: explore, hide unexplored hexes, move the party. Spectator: Settings → “Spectator access” → link or QR code to share.

14. New in 5.6

  1. Wild Shape and Polymorph: sheet → In play → “🐾 Transform”; pick an SRD beast (or a custom form). 2014 rules: the form has its own HP, damage hits it first and, at 0 HP, the character reverts and takes the excess. 2024 rules: temporary HP (level for Wild Shape, the beast’s HP for Polymorph). “Return to my form” ends it.
  2. Containers: in an item’s form, “Capacity if a container” (and “Contents weigh nothing” for a bag of holding); the SRD catalogue sets them for the backpack, sack, pouch, chest, bag of holding and handy haversack. Each item then offers “Store in”. Lights: “🔥 Light” on a torch, candle or flask of oil in the inventory: one is used up, the light burns down as world time passes, and the bearer’s token lights the map. Day and night: Maps → “👁 Token vision” → “Follow world time” lighting.
  3. Weather: Chronicle → Calendar → “☁ Today’s weather”; descriptive only. Health states: when a creature’s HP are hidden, players see Unhurt, Wounded, Bloodied, Near death or Out of the fight; checkbox in the initiative order.

15. New in 5.7

  1. Hosting on another server: HOSTING.en.md (in the Chroniques folder) explains how to run Chroniques on a rented Linux server, a mini PC or behind a Cloudflare Tunnel. The templates in hebergement/ (Caddy, nginx, systemd service) are ready to copy. To bring your campaigns over: node scripts/migrate-data.js <old folder>. Behind Caddy or nginx, set CHRONIQUES_PROXY=standard.
  2. Players: on Android, Settings → “Change server”; in a browser or on iPhone, open the new address and add the icon again. If the domain changes, passkeys must be created again (sign in with the password).

16. New in 5.8

  1. Clocks: Today’s session → “⏳ Clocks”. A circle of 4 to 12 segments for an approaching threat or a project moving forward; fill one segment at each step. Visible to players (home page, table screen, spectator) or secret; the note stays with the GM.
  2. Stars and wishes: Chronicle → Sessions → “Ask for feedback”. From their home page, each player writes a star (what they enjoyed) and a wish (what they’d like to see). In anonymous mode nobody knows who wrote what, not even the GM.
  3. Investigation board: Chronicle → Investigation. The GM prepares clues (people, places, objects, events, questions), reveals and links them; the GM note is never shown. Players see revealed clues and write their theories there.
  4. Maps: “✏ Draw” (freehand or arrow, five colours) for everyone; each person erases their own strokes and the GM can erase everything. Token altitude (flying, levitation, pit) in the token panel, shown on the token.
